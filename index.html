Questions

What counts as an update?

Changing text, swapping photos, updating prices or adding a promo banner. Building new pages or a new feature is quoted separately.

Do unused hours roll over?

No. The 3 hours reset at the start of each month. That keeps the plan simple and keeps replies fast for everyone.

Can I have the monthly plan without a new build?

Yes, if you already have a site I can work with. Send me the address and I'll check it before we start.

Why a flat price?

You know what you'll pay before the month starts, and I don't have to invoice you for every small change.
